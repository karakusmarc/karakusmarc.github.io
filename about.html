Marc Karakus Pérez

From engineering to yacht design, an evolving interest in architecture and life at sea.

Born and raised in Mallorca, the sea has always been a natural part of my life. Growing up surrounded by the Mediterranean and its maritime culture, I developed a fascination with boats and life on the water.

Towards the end of my engineering degree, I wanted to take my career in a different direction. Yacht design offered the opportunity to bring together my passion for the sea with my interests in engineering, architecture and design.

My experiences in Milan and South Africa have been personally and professionally very enriching.

Living and working in different environments has broadened my perspective, strengthened my interest in yacht design and reassured me that this is the path I want to pursue.

For now, my ambition is to keep learning, gaining experience and developing my own approach to design. In the longer term, I hope to establish my own yacht design studio in Mallorca, returning to the place where my connection with the sea first began.

Beyond design, I find inspiration in the simple things: reading, travelling, staying active and spending time outdoors, particularly by the sea. These moments allow me to disconnect, discover new perspectives and nurture the curiosity that has always guided both my personal and professional journey.
